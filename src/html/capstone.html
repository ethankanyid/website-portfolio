About ▼

The Smart Rack Monitoring Platform is a senior capstone project built for TELECSO, a Colombian networking company. The product monitors server rack conditions in real time — tracking things like temperature, humidity, and whether a rack door is open — and displays everything in a web dashboard. Our team was split into sections: hardware, backend, frontend, and cloud. I specifically worked on the cloud infrastructure and DevOps side, handling how the app was built, deployed, and kept running. The project placed 2nd at the WSU School of Engineering and Applied Sciences capstone showcase and is now being marketed in Colombia.
