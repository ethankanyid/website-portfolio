About ▼

As part of WSU’s CySER research program in Spring 2026, our team studied how images can be subtly altered to fool AI models into giving wrong or harmful responses. My role was to test different attack methods — specifically FOA Attack, M-Attack, and V-Attack — run them on WSU’s supercomputer (Kamiak), and produce results for the rest of the team. FOA was chosen as our primary method and generated the adversarial images the team then analyzed. The rest of the team worked with those images and put together the research poster.
